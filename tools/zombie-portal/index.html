    How it works

    1. Same Zombie, now fully onchain. Zombies #1–#1117 (the 999 Zombies plus the villains, heroes, Zombie Punks and other artifacts) have their art on a file host today. The portal stores the exact same pixels and traits inside a contract on Base, so they live as long as Base does.
    2. Traits, cleaned up. The portal fixes the collection's old spelling: every trait starts with a capital letter, duplicates spelled two ways become one (gold chain and Gold Chain → Gold Chain), typos are fixed (Earing → Earring, mustach → Mustache), every animated Zombie gets Animated: Yes, and Zombie Punks get Background: Not For Sale. Nothing about your Zombie's look changes.
    3. Same name, new token ID. Zombie 5 is still called Zombie 5 after the portal. Only its token ID changes, from #5 to #1,000,005: the old token is burned and the new one is minted to you in the same transaction. Your traits stay; a glitch only adds traits (and Animate changes Animated to Yes).
    4. Clean is free. Glitched is 0.0006 ETH. Animated is 0.0025 ETH, the original Zombie mint price. Mix up to eight glitch effects with the sliders, each from 0 to 100, then reroll the placement until it feels right. Static Zombies get the glitch baked into their pixels, or pay 0.0025 ETH to make it animated, like minting a new Zombie (their Animated trait becomes yes). Animated Zombies get animated glitches on top. Your mix is locked forever.
    5. One new trait: Glitch: Yes. A glitched Zombie gets the trait Glitch: Yes (and Animated: Yes if you made it animated). All your original traits stay. The exact recipe, every effect's strength, the speed and the placement, is written in the token's description, so you can always see how it was made.
    6. What you see is what you get. The preview above is drawn by the same code the contract runs, and you can double-check it against the contract before you go.
    7. Nobody can fake the art. Every Zombie's original art and traits are fingerprinted in the contract (a Merkle root). The portal rejects anything that doesn't match.
    8. Open until December 31, 2026, then holders keep it alive. After the year ends the portal closes by itself. Any holder of a Zombie #1–#1117 can reopen it for everyone for 24 hours by paying 0.0006 ETH, and it closes again by itself. The artist can pause it at any time.
    9. Every Zombie from #1 to #1117. Zombies #1118 and up are already fully onchain. The artifacts keep every frame of their animation as pixel art (without their film grain), and every Zombie keeps its name and description.

    Burning can't be undone. Any listing of the original token ends, and marketplaces show the new token as a new item.
